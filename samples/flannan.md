# The Flannan Isles Mystery
thumb: 3 KEEPERS VANISHED

## The Dark Light
card: The Dark Light
visuals: Flannan Isles lighthouse | Eilean Mor | Outer Hebrides map

> Wide shot of a lonely white lighthouse on top of a steep green island, grey stormy sea all around, 1900
Far off the west coast of Scotland, past the Isle of Lewis, lies a group of tiny islands called the Flannan Isles.
> Wide shot of three Scottish lighthouse keepers in dark navy wool uniforms and peaked caps standing at the lighthouse door, one with a grey beard
On the biggest one, Eilean Mor, a new lighthouse had been shining for just over a year. Three men lived there. James Ducat, the principal keeper. Thomas Marshall, his assistant. And Donald McArthur, a stand in for a keeper who was off sick.

> Wide shot of a steamship at night on a dark sea, a lighthouse on a distant island with no light
On the night of the fifteenth of December, 1900, a steamer passed the islands. Its crew saw something strange. The lighthouse was dark.
> Wide shot of fishermen in wool jumpers on the shore of Lewis looking out to sea at night
They reported it when they reached port, but the message never reached the lighthouse board. And for days, nobody on Lewis saw the light either.

## The Empty Station
card: The Empty Station
visuals: lighthouse tender ship | lighthouse keeper 1900 | Hesperus ship

> Wide shot of a small Victorian steamship fighting big waves toward a rocky island, 1900
Storms kept the relief ship away. On the twenty sixth of December, the ship Hesperus finally reached the island. Its captain blew the ship's whistle and fired a flare. No answer came.
> Wide shot of a young lighthouse keeper in a navy uniform climbing stone steps up a cliff alone, grey sky
Joseph Moore, a relief keeper, climbed up to the station. Nobody came to meet him.
> Wide shot of a tidy Victorian lighthouse kitchen with an empty table, a hook on the wall with one oilskin coat
Inside, the lamps were cleaned and ready to light. The kitchen was tidy. But two sets of oilskins were gone, and one was still hanging on its hook.
> Wide shot of a lighthouse logbook and a writing slate on a wooden desk by a window, stormy sea outside
The last log entry was from the thirteenth. Notes on a slate ran until the morning of the fifteenth. Then nothing. Three men had vanished.
> Wide shot of a Victorian ship captain with a white beard writing a telegram in a small cabin, oil lamp
The captain sent a telegram to the mainland. A dreadful accident has happened at the Flannans.

## The Answer
card: The Answer
visuals: west landing cliffs | Atlantic storm waves | Flannan Isles cliffs

> Wide shot of a Victorian official in a long dark coat studying twisted iron railings on a cliff edge, huge waves below
The superintendent, Robert Muirhead, came to investigate. At the west landing, he found iron railings twisted out of shape. A block of stone weighing more than a ton had been torn loose.
> Wide shot of two small keepers in oilskins on a cliff landing, a giant wave rising from the sea behind them
The men had put on the clothes they wore to go down to the landings. Muirhead believed a huge wave rose up and swept them into the sea.
> Wide shot of a ghostly lighthouse at night under a full moon, mist rolling over the island
Years later, a poem and magazine stories added an overturned chair, an untouched meal and strange log entries about crying men. None of that is in the official records.
> Wide shot of a lighthouse beam sweeping over a calm sea at dawn
The real story is simpler, and just as chilling. Three men went out to check on their island in a storm, and the sea never gave them back.
