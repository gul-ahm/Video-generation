## First Chapter Name

> Wide shot of a lonely lighthouse on a green island, grey stormy sea all around, 1900
The narration starts here. Write the words exactly as the narrator should say them. One picture line covers the one
or two sentences below it, until the next picture line.
> Wide shot of three lighthouse keepers in dark navy wool uniforms and peaked caps at the lighthouse door
Every line that starts with a ">" is a picture. Describe what we see, the people, their clothes and the year. Keep
people adults unless the story is about a child.

A blank line starts a new paragraph. The voice takes a short breath there.

## Second Chapter Name

You can also leave out every picture line in a chapter, like this one. Then Gemini writes the pictures for it,
one picture for about every forty words.

Numbers are fine as digits or words. Write years as digits, like 1872. Dates like the fourth of December read best
in words.
